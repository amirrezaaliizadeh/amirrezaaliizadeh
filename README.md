## What I'm working on

- 🎓 MSc thesis on consumer behavior and the sustainability-consumption paradox
- 📄 Research on machine-readable product disclosure (schema.org markup of Italian running-shoe brands)
- 🎙️ [Sinekhiz](https://amirrezaalizadeh.com/podcast), a Persian-language podcast

## Background

About six years of work experience in Iran: sports sponsorship negotiation, sales management at a smart-building automation company, running a business school, and media and communications. Now based in Milan.
